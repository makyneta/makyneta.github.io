Website for the Marinha Grande Athletics Club

Clube Atletismo de Marinha Grande 2 months

Overview & Purpose

This project aims to increase the digital reach of the Marinha Grande Athletics Club. The website has three sub-sections: the club section, containing information about the club, governing bodies, national champions, statistics, and newsletter; registration for membership and membership, followed by the regulations; and tastings offered and produced by the club, followed by news, a gallery, and contact information. All website development was carried out in conjunction with members of the club's board, including the president and vice-president in their respective roles at the time of creation. The website also includes legal information such as terms and conditions, privacy policy, cookie policy, and a complaints book – along with a website map.
