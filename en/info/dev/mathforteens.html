Math For Teens

Tomás Correia 2 months

Overview & Goal

This project aims to expand the digital reach of Math For Teens, a project created by Tomás Correia that seeks to teach mathematics to teenagers in a fun and engaging way. The website offers a variety of educational resources, including tutorials, practical exercises, and explanatory videos, all designed to make learning mathematics more accessible and interesting for young students. It includes an About page, a Contact page with a contact form, a Services page describing the services offered and where visitors can get in touch directly through the form. It also includes a video lessons section, which is automatically added from the YouTube API. Next, the Testimonials page includes a book section where people can add their own testimonial directly on the site. To finish, it features a store section, created in partnership with Payhip, fully integrated into the site, with products added automatically from the Payhip API.
