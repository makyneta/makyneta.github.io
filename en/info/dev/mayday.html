Website for the Musical Band, MAYDAY

MAYDAY, Nickz, Shin, Léo Lr, Yago Tiger 7 days

Overview & Purpose

This project consists of creating a professional website with a musical style for the Brazilian band MAYDAY, to share their musical repertoire, upcoming concerts, and other information about the band. The goal is to gather all their content in one easy-to-access location, with a professional and minimalist style, including a notification whenever MAYDAY is on tour.
